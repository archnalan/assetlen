# Dinah — The Second Principal

*Peter's wife and co-client. Local. Walks past the site. Owns every finish decision.*

> Grounded in [whatsapp-evidence.md](whatsapp-evidence.md) — the HOUSE PLAN thread.
> **This persona did not exist in the original vision documents.** It was added
> 2026-08-12 after reading a real year-long project thread, in which she is the second
> most active participant and the source of most spec churn.

---

## Why she exists in the model

The vision assumed one client. The real project has two, with **different jobs, different
appetites, and different failure modes**:

| | Peter | Dinah |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Abroad / travelling | Local, near the site |
| Cares about | Progress, money, dates | Layouts, finishes, colours, fittings |
| Reads a photo for | *Did the thing I funded move?* | *Is that what I chose?* |
| Chases when | Silent for 48 hours | She physically sees no one working |
| Reverses decisions | Rarely | Routinely, and says so |

A single undifferentiated "Client channel" serves one of them badly. That is a design
constraint on the Client Brief, not a nice-to-have.

---

## What she actually does in the thread

- **She is the accidental clerk of works.** *"There's no one at the site yet."* (7 Dec)
  *"Place is quiet."* *"It's like only 3 guys work these days."* (5 Feb) She reports site
  reality to the group — the exact role the vision assigns to a clerk who does not exist.
- **She owns the interior spec.** Washroom removed from the first-floor common room
  (21 Nov). Master washroom door returned to the closet (23 Nov). Balconies deleted
  (25 May, *"those small balconies are a waste of space"*). Gypsum ceiling demanded
  (7 Jul, *"Let's have a modern house please"*). Burglar-bar design rejected on delivery
  (3 Aug, *"There's even a cross in the middle"*).
- **She reverses herself, and knows it.** *"Sorry I remember I told you to change it."*
  (23 Nov) This is not carelessness — it is what design iteration looks like when it lives
  in a chat. It is also exactly the case that a versioned, attributed commitment fixes.
- **She has cost opinions and no cost visibility.** *"A boys quarter for 300m is too much."*
  (20 May) *"Honestly what amount are we going to pay for only retaining wall in that
  place."* (11 Jun) She is reacting to numbers she has no register to check.
- **She escalates schedule pressure emotionally, not formally.** *"It's dragging already
  and not matching with how urgent I was needed here."* (7 Dec)

---

## What she needs

| Need | Form |
|---|---|
| **See what she chose, as she chose it** | Every finish commitment carries the image she approved and the date. Reversals supersede, never overwrite. |
| **Change her mind cheaply and see the consequence** | *"Removing this washroom shrinks the BQ room and adds window W05"* — the cascade shown at the moment of the change, not discovered on site five months later. |
| **A price on an opinion** | When she says *"too much"*, the number and what it buys should be one tap away. |
| **A brief weighted to her** | Same facts as Peter's, different emphasis: what was fitted, what she owes a choice on, what arrives next. |

---

## What she must never be given

She is a **Client**, not a Manager. She sees the Client Brief and the commitments on her
projects. She does not see the Site Log — the unsanitised operational record is precisely
what Nalan needs a private surface for, and *"if I add the clerk to the group he posts
things the client shouldn't see raw"* applies to her too.

---

## Failure conditions

Assetlen has failed Dinah if:

- She has to walk to the site to find out whether anyone is working.
- A finish she chose is fitted differently and there is no record of the choice.
- Reversing a decision requires re-explaining the whole context.
- Her brief is Peter's brief with her name on it.

---

## The one sentence

**Dinah wants the house to be what she chose — and to be able to prove, months later, that
she chose it.**
