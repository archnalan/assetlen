# Peter — The Developer

*Property developer. Several projects live at once. Rarely on site.*

> Grounded in [whatsapp-evidence.md](whatsapp-evidence.md) — the HOUSE PLAN thread,
> 18 Sep 2025 → 4 Aug 2026, 1,529 messages. Dates below are citations from that export.

---

## Who he is

Peter funds and directs residential development. At any moment he has two or three
projects running at different stages — on this engagement alone: the **main house**, the
**guest wing**, **external works**, and a separate **office building in another town**, all
built by the same person. He is not a construction professional and does not want to become one.
He runs his projects on trust, a verbal agreement, and a phone.

His arrangement with [Nalan](Nalan.md) is deliberately informal: they agree a stage, Peter
funds it up front, and Nalan designs and builds it. There is no quantity surveyor, no
contract administrator, no monthly valuation meeting. Peter does not want that machinery.
He wants to know that the stage he paid for is being built the way they agreed.

He is also **structurally absent**. He is in another country for most of the build,
mobilising resources, in summits, clearing containers. He funds through **three named
agents**, across two banks and mobile money, into an account that is not even in the
contractor's name. His only instrument is the phone.

Today that knowledge arrives through a WhatsApp group.

---

## The second principal

**Peter is not the only client-side reader.** His wife, [Dinah](Dinah.md), is local,
walks past the site, and owns every interior and finish decision. She reports site
conditions to the group that Peter cannot see (*"Place is quiet"*, *"It's like only 3 guys
work these days"*), and she reverses her own instructions.

The two of them want **different briefs from the same facts**: Peter wants progress and
money; Dinah wants specs, colours and layouts. A single undifferentiated "Client channel"
serves one of them badly. Read [Dinah.md](Dinah.md) before designing the Client Brief.

---

## What he is actually doing when he opens his phone

Peter is not browsing photos. He is doing one of four things, and all four are the same
underlying act:

1. Looking for a **promise document** — the quote, the receipt, the invoice he already cleared.
2. Looking for a **photo that proves a promise** — what the wall looked like when they agreed it.
3. Looking for a **material that was agreed** — the tile, the finish, the spec.
4. Wanting to **sketch on top of an image and ask a question** — circle the thing, ask why.

In each case he is holding a past commitment up against present reality.

> **Peter does not need a message archive. He needs a commitments register.**

WhatsApp stores messages. Messages have positions in a stream, and positions expire the
moment new messages arrive. So Peter has no way to point at anything — he can only find it
again and re-send it. That is why, on 23 January, he wrote: *"Can you reshare receipts for
payment made so far. I am arranging funds today and I want us to be up to date with our
records."* Those receipts had already been sent.

## The daily loop he wants

- Wakes up somewhere else.
- Opens one page. Reads it in under a minute.
- Sees what moved yesterday, what is blocked, what he owes a decision on.
- Occasionally opens a photo, circles something, asks a question.
- Closes the phone.

He does not want to request the update. He does not want to chase Nalan. The update
should arrive whether or not anyone remembered to send it.

**This is the single best-evidenced need in the whole thread.** Peter asked for it in
writing twice —

> *"Let me have daily updates with pictorials."* — 25 Jan
> *"I would appreciate daily progress updates."* — 11 Feb

— and still had to chase seventeen more times: *"How is today's progress at the
site"*, *"Did you start with the block works?"*, *"Any progress updates and what is
expected next so that I can prepare"*, *"What is going on with the retaining wall?"*,
*"what of works at the main building"*. A standing request that two adults both want and
neither can sustain is not a discipline problem. It is a missing system.

## What Peter values in an MVP

| Need | What it looks like | Thread evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **A glance, not a scroll** | One daily brief, organised by the work he is paying for — not forty photos in posting order. | 25 Feb: 17 photos posted in one minute; Peter's reply — *"Nothing much changed."* |
| **Evidence he can trust** | Camera-captured, timestamped, same vantage point over time. Day 1 / Day 8 / Day 15 of the same corner beats 40 random shots. | Seventeen chase messages follow days on which photos *were* posted. Volume is not the problem; comparability is. |
| **A funded-vs-claimed ledger** | Stage funded X, claimed to date Y, variations Z, evidence attached. Nothing more sophisticated. | 29 Jul: *"That is confusing me… Too many stages combined. I want to know if they were cleared or not."* Settled only by meeting in person. |
| **A variations register** | What changed, why, cost delta, approved yes/no. | An entire extra floor added 30 Jan (*"let me know the additional cost"*); balconies removed 25 May (*"saved approximately 15M"*); labour quoted at 12M and rejected 11 Jun. |
| **A decisions queue** | *"Burglar-bar design needed or the fabricator stops."* One list, with consequences attached. This is his real anxiety — not photos. | Doors and windows shipped from China Feb → Jun; *"Plastering waiting on the doors and windows"* (8 May) blocked finishing for weeks. |
| **Findability months later** | *"What did I approve on the balustrade?"* answered in one search, including things that were only ever pictures. | 3 Aug burglar-bar dispute resolved by hunting down an image sent weeks earlier. |
| **The right to reopen** | A cleared item can still be questioned without it feeling like an accusation. | 6 Jun: Peter queries transport on a settled partition bill. Nalan's answer is a message, and dissolves. |

## What frustrates him today

- **Endless repeated content.** He re-sends and re-requests receipts because there is
  nowhere to point. On 19 December he forwarded **nine deposit slips in four minutes** to
  establish a balance both parties should already have shared.
- **Screenshots and markup that orphan themselves.** He circles a line on a screenshot; the
  markup is now a new image with no connection to the original document.
- **Images are unsearchable.** The reason he scrolls is that WhatsApp cannot read a receipt
  or a BoQ page.
- **Answers dissolve.** Nalan answers the question, but the answer is a message, so next
  month it is the next thing Peter has to scroll for.
- **Money reconciles only in person.** Two of the largest financial conversations on this
  project ended with *"let us sit"* and a physical meeting.
- **Ideas die.** External works and the boundary wall were parked in February pending a
  conversation with a neighbour; they resurface only when someone happens to remember.
- **The record edits itself underneath him.** Fourteen edited messages, ten deleted. On
  21 January a typo (*"I haven't seen the beauty in the first proposal"* → *"I meant this
  new proposal doesn't look good at all. It was a typo."*) inverted a design verdict for
  nine hours.
- **One volume dial.** Either he drowns in conduit photos or he is in the dark. There is no
  middle setting — and no setting at all that distinguishes what he needs from what Dinah
  needs.

## Peter's trust condition

Peter will accept a filtered feed — he does not want everything — **but only if he is told
the rule.** Say it once, plainly:

> *You see the things that matter: money, dates, specs, blockers, decisions you owe, and
> evidence on stages you funded. You do not see the ordinary volume of site life. Nalan
> controls emphasis. He does not control those facts.*

A curated feed he knows is curated beats a raw feed he would never read.

---

## Failure conditions

Assetlen has failed Peter if:

- The brief does not arrive when Nalan is busy or absent.
- He has to go somewhere to find decisions he owes.
- He asks a question and cannot tell, later, whether it was answered.
- He cannot see funded-vs-claimed for a stage without asking for it.
- A parked idea resurfaces too late to act on.
- A decision reached on a phone call leaves no trace.
- Anything requires more taps than sending a photo in WhatsApp.

---

## The one sentence

**Peter wants to hold a past commitment up against present reality — in seconds, from
anywhere, without asking anyone.**
