# Evidence — the HOUSE PLAN thread

**Source:** `WhatsApp Chat with HOUSE PLAN.txt` — a real, complete, year-long project
group. **18 Sep 2025 → 4 Aug 2026. 1,529 messages.** Exported without media; every
`<Media omitted>` marker is preserved, so volume and position are intact even where the
image is not.

This file exists so the personas and the phase plan are argued from a real project instead
of from intuition. Every claim in [Peter.md](Peter.md), [Nalan.md](Nalan.md) and
[Dinah.md](Dinah.md) traces to a line here. It is also the input corpus for
[assetlen.md](assetlen.md) §9 test 1 — the extraction test.

**Do not paraphrase this file into the plan. Cite it.**

> ### Anonymity
>
> **Client identities are masked.** *Peter* and *Dinah* are pseudonyms; surnames, third-party
> names, bank names, account numbers, the town, the district and the site location have all
> been removed or generalised. Product brands, quantities, dates, amounts and quoted
> sentences are kept verbatim because they are what the extraction model has to work on.
> Line numbers refer to the private export, which is **not** in this repository.
>
> **Do not reintroduce a real full name, bank, account or place into any file in this repo**
> — including seed data, demo fixtures and screenshots.

### F2 — Volume without comparability communicates nothing

25 Feb 2026: Nalan posts **17 photos at 17:18** (lines 630–647). Peter, 46 minutes later
(lines 648–650):

> *"How is today's progress?"* … *"Nothing much changed."*

Seventeen frames in posting order conveyed less than one same-vantage-point pair would
have. **Grouped-by-deliverable with a fixed vantage point is not an aesthetic preference;
it is the difference between a signal and noise.**

### F3 — Money reconciles only in person

The whole arc:

- **19 Dec** (307–317) — Peter forwards **nine deposit slips in four minutes** to establish
  a balance both parties should already share.
- **23 Jan** (374–382) — *"Can you reshare receipts for payment made so far. I am arranging
  funds today and I want us to be up to date with our records."* Nalan: *"[the account
  holder], whose account we are using, has noted that a deposit was made recently… We have
  not yet clarified how much."*
- **6 Feb** (496–502) — *"confirm if you received the UGX 71,000,000"* → *"issue a receipt
  and reconcile."*
- **26 Feb** (676) — *"issue a receipt and carry the balance towards the next stage."*
- **29 Jul** (1437–1469) — the blow-up:

  > Peter: *"Let me look into the original bill and check on payments cleared first."*
  > Peter: *"What is the variation so that I don't get confused."*
  > Peter: *"That is confusing me. We need to go step by step. Too many stages combined.
  > I want to know if they were cleared or not."*
  > Peter: *"Yes, we need to sit and pick out the variations. On every phase."*

  Resolved by scheduling a 9 AM physical meeting the next morning.

> **What was missing was never a payment rail.** It was a per-stage ledger:
> **funded → claimed → cleared → carried forward**, with variations itemised. assetlen.md
> §7 is right to refuse to move money and right to record that it moved.

### F5 — Decisions are made on calls and in person, and leave no trace

> *"Let me call you we discuss the options"* — 24 Feb (627)
> *"I will call you once I get out of the summit that I am in for now"* — 11 Jun (1099)
> *"Nalan can we meet for this please?"* — 30 Jan (429)
> *"Let me come 2pm and we reconcile this"* → meeting moved to 9 AM — 29 Jul (1464–1476)

**The highest-value commitments on this project were agreed where the thread cannot see
them.** No phase of the current plan addresses this. See §6.

### F6 — The record is mutable, and mutation changes meaning

Fourteen edits, ten deletions. The clearest case, 21 Jan (366–372):

> 01:31 Peter: *"Obviously I haven't seen the beauty in the first proposal"*
> 07:05 Dinah: *"What are you talking about please?"*
> 09:42 Nalan: *"Do you think we can proceed to pursue this idea with visible roof?"*
> 10:49 Peter: *"I meant this new proposal doesn't look good at all"* / *"It was a typo"*

A typo inverted a design verdict for nine hours and pulled two people into resolving it.
An append-only commitment with an explicit state cannot be typo'd into its opposite.

### F7 — Artifacts leak out of the system

20 Nov (124–128): Dinah — *"It's not opening."* Nalan — *"Would a video open?"* … *"Sent
it to email."* Nalan's email address is itself posted into the thread (line 34).

Drawings, BoQs and schedules therefore live in **two** systems, with no shared address.
This is Law 2's justification in the wild.

## 6. The gap no phase currently covers

**Verbal decisions.** F5 shows the largest commitments — the labour rate, the additional
floor's cost, the finishes reconciliation, the roof-vs-slab choice — were settled on a
phone call or across a table. WhatsApp does not capture them. **Neither does the current
plan.** Nothing in P2–P9 gives Nalan a way to write down *"agreed on the call: labour for
the retaining wall at UGX Xm, Peter, 11 Jun"* in less time than it takes to forget it.

Proposed shape, deliberately small:

- One field, one tap, from anywhere: **"Log a decision."**
- It creates a Commitment at `Agreed`, attributed to both parties, timestamped, marked
  `SourceChannel = Verbal`.
- The counterparty sees it in the next brief with **Confirm** / **That's not what we said**.
- Disputes flip it to `QueryRaised`. It never silently becomes truth.

This is the cheapest high-value item the corpus reveals. It should land with P3, not later.
